About this demo

This demonstration loads no analytics, advertising pixels, external fonts or third-party scripts. It stores the consent choice you make in this browser using cc_consent_v1 in local storage. The record includes analytics and marketing choices, region, language, version and timestamps.

The demo simulates permission signals; accepting a category does not activate a real tracking tool. Use “Reset saved demo choice” on the home page to clear the saved record.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process connection information; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Project profiles and examples use synthetic business details.

The customizer keeps business details in browser memory and includes them only in the files you choose to download. Its preview uses a separate consent record, cc_customizer_preview; clear site storage to remove it. It does not overwrite the main demo choice or transmit your business details to a server.

The guided setup also keeps questionnaire answers in browser memory. Saving a project or preparing a lawyer packet downloads those details to your device. No lawyer receives them automatically. The installable WordPress plugin excludes the private questionnaire and contact email. Entering a website URL does not trigger a remote scan.

This explanation covers the demonstration only. It is not a policy template for your business or legal advice.
